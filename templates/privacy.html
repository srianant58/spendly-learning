Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate and improve Spendly: authenticating your account, displaying your expense history, generating spending summaries, and providing customer support. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which we will remove it within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party services for functions such as hosting, email delivery, or font delivery. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app. We're happy to help.
